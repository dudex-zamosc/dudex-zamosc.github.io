Floor sanding is the process of mechanically removing the old finish and smoothing the wood surface. It is a crucial step in floor restoration that brings back the original beauty of your hardwood floor and prepares it for a new protective coating. Regular sanding can extend the lifespan of your floor by decades.

If your floor has lost its former shine, or you can see scratches, discoloration, or unevenness — it's time for professional sanding. With our experience and modern dust-free equipment, we'll restore the beautiful, natural look of your hardwood floor.

Our Sanding Process

1

Inspection and Estimate

We visit your property, assess the condition of the floor, wood type, and scope of work. Based on this, we prepare a free, detailed estimate — with no hidden costs.

2

Surface Preparation

We protect furniture and fixtures. We check and repair loose blocks, fill gaps, and remove protruding nails and other obstacles.

3

Machine Sanding

We use professional drum and rotary sanders with a dust-free system. This ensures clean and comfortable working conditions — dust is captured directly at the source.

4

Finish Sanding

After the main sanding, we move on to fine-grit sanding to achieve a perfectly smooth surface. This is a crucial step that determines the final visual result.

5

Varnishing or Oiling

Finally, we apply the chosen protective finish — varnish, oil, or oil-wax. We select the product based on the wood type and your preferences.

Types of Floors We Sand

Classic Parquet

Traditional parquet blocks laid in herringbone, basketweave, and other patterns. Sanding restores their original character.

Engineered Wood Flooring

Popular three-layer boards. We sand them with care, protecting the thin top layer of real wood.

Mosaic Parquet

Small wooden elements creating decorative patterns. They require precise sanding and careful finishing.

Solid Wood Flooring

Solid planks of oak, ash, beech, and other species. Ideal for multiple sanding cycles over their lifetime.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should hardwood floors be sanded? +

The frequency depends on how heavily the floor is used. For residential floors, we recommend sanding every 8–15 years, while commercial spaces may need it every 5–8 years. Regular maintenance and proper care can significantly extend these intervals.

How long does floor sanding take? +

The timeline depends on the floor area and its condition. A standard apartment (40–60 m²) typically takes 2–3 working days, including varnishing. We'll provide an accurate schedule during the initial inspection.

Is floor sanding noisy and messy? +

We use professional dust-free equipment that captures over 95% of dust directly at the source. While the process does generate some noise, we make every effort to minimize disruption and ensure thorough cleanup after the work is done.

How much does floor sanding cost? +

The cost depends on several factors: floor area, condition, wood type, and chosen finish. We offer free on-site inspections and estimates — contact us and we'll prepare a detailed quote tailored to your needs.
